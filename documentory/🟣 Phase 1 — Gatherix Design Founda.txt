🟣 Phase 1 — Gatherix Design Foundation

Before making the Navbar or Home page, we need to establish the visual language of the website.

Our initial design direction:

Primary: Indigo / Purple
Background: White + very light gray
Text: Dark navy
Accent: Violet/Pink
Cards: White with subtle borders/shadows
Corners: Rounded, but not excessive
Typography: Clean modern sans-serif
Style: Professional + energetic + minimal
